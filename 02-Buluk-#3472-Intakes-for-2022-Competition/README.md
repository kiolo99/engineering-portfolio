**Manufacturing:** The core structure of both intakes was completely done of MDF as a prototype, adapting all the measurements to current existing pieces that are intended to use. None of these two designs were used in the final robot going to the competition

## Overview

As part of the 2022 FIRST Robotics Competition, I was responsible for designing and prototyping two different intake mechanisms for the Buluk #3472 robotics team. Using CAD, I developed two design concepts and manufactured physical prototypes to evaluate and compare their functionality.

The goal was to explore different ways of collecting match balls and transferring them into the robot's chassis.

**Software:** Onshape

## Project Details

**Objective:** Design two alternative intake mechanisms, create CAD models, and manufacture physical prototypes to evaluate their functionality.

**My Contribution:** Developed two intake designs as part of a two-person team, from CAD modeling to physical prototyping.

**Design Process:** The mechanical team began with a brainstorming session to generate different concepts for the robot's intake mechanism. The proposed designs were then distributed among teams of two. My teammate and I were responsible for developing the two concepts presented below.

**Manufacturing and Testing:** Both prototypes used MDF for their main structural components. Their dimensions were adapted to fit existing parts intended for the robot. The physical prototypes allowed us to evaluate the designs and their functionality. Neither design was ultimately selected for the final competition robot.
